Help & about

How your goal is worked out

Your baseline is 35 ml per kg of body weight, scaled by your activity level, plus 300 ml in pregnancy or 700 ml while breastfeeding. On hot, humid or high-UV days the weather model adds up to 1 litre more.

Privacy

Everything you log stays in this browser on this device. Only your location's coordinates are sent, to the weather service, to fetch the forecast.
